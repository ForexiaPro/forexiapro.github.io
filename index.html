COT report data, a position size calculator, a currency strength meter and session clock, a trading journal, a Discord bot and a browser toolbar, from Forexia Academy. Each one works on its own, with no account and no key.

Data

COT report data

The CFTC's Commitments of Traders report for FX futures and gold as clean CSV and JSON, updated each Friday: net positions, the change on the week and net as a % of open interest. Public domain data.
